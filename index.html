About

Vishal Mehta.

I’m a UX design leader and strategist with 25+ years of experience spanning software engineering, design systems, and AI-augmented product workflows. I founded IDYeah Studio to bring a different standard of craft to founders and product teams who deserve more than average.

My practice sits at the intersection of design craft and business strategy. I don’t just make things look good — I help teams figure out what they’re actually saying, and how to say it with precision.

Published author, ACM SIGCHI member, US Green Card for Extraordinary Ability (EB1A), and speaker at the World Usability Congress. My AI-augmented workflow ships production-ready React and design systems at a pace most teams don’t expect.
