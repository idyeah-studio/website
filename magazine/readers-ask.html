The audit

What is a product audit?

A product audit is an independent review of your product or app, read from the outside in. I look at it the way your buyer does, then tell you what works, what gets in the way, and what to fix first.

It is for founders and product teams who sense something is off and can’t name it, agencies who want an outside read on a client’s product, and sales professionals who need to understand a prospect before they write a proposal.

What is the brand audit?

The deeper read. It covers everything in the product audit, then adds your website, your deck and your social presence, through the same eight lenses.

It takes 5 to 7 days and costs $1,500.

What are the eight lenses?

UX, Visual, Copy, Brand, Marketing, Industry, Buyer and Investor. Each one is a different expert’s chair, so the same screen gets read eight ways.

UX asks whether it works. Visual asks whether it looks considered. Copy asks whether the words are clear. Brand asks whether it sounds like one company. Marketing asks whether the right people will find it. Industry asks how it stands in its field. Buyer asks whether someone would pay. Investor asks whether someone would back it.

What do I get, and how soon?

A written report: in 2 to 3 days for a product audit, and in 5 to 7 days for a brand audit. It starts with what already works, ranks every finding by severity, and ends with decisions in order: what I saw, why it matters, and what to do.

You can read a full sample audit on the Crit8 page.

How is it different from other audits?

Most expert audits look through one lens, usually UX, and take a week or more. Quick video reviews are faster, but cover a single page or flow. AI tools are instant, and leave the judgment to you.

Mine reads your product through eight lenses in one report, in 2 to 3 days. And I make the calls myself: what matters most, and what to do first.

What does an audit cost?

Both prices are fixed: $950 for a product audit, $1,500 for a brand audit. Each covers all eight lenses and the full written report.

If you start a product design project with me within 30 days, I credit half the audit fee toward it.

Design

Three kinds of work. Product: apps and design systems, designed in code. Deck: pitch decks, story and slides. Brand: your product, website, deck and social presence as one identity.

Do I need an audit before design work?

No. Many projects start with one because it shows what to fix first, and half the audit fee is credited toward the product design that follows.

Brand design, from $6,000, covers product, web, deck and social in 3 to 4 weeks.

It has a 7-day free trial with no card needed. See Crit8, or start at crit8.ai.

Who uses Crit8?

Founders and product teams auditing their own work. Agencies reviewing client projects. Sales professionals researching a prospect before they submit a proposal.

Crit8 is self-serve: you run the audit and judge the findings yourself. With an audit from me, I read your product personally, decide what matters most, and tell you where to start.
