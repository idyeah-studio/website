Privacy.

Last updated 12 Sep 2026

The short version: this site sets no cookies, shows no ads, and sells no data. There is nothing to accept.

Analytics

Traffic is measured with Umami, a privacy-focused, cookieless analytics service. It counts page views and interactions in aggregate, without cookies, fingerprinting, or personal identifiers, and it respects your browser's Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals. If either is enabled, nothing is measured at all.

Your theme choice

The light or dark theme toggle stores your preference in your browser's local storage. It stays on your device and is never transmitted anywhere.

External services

Booking a call runs through Calendly, and writing to the studio uses your own mail app. Links to LinkedIn, Medium, and X lead to those platforms. Each of these services has its own privacy policy that applies once you are there.

Email

If you write to vishal@idyeah.studio, your message is used to reply to you and for nothing else. It is not added to any list.

Questions

Write to vishal@idyeah.studio. You will get an answer from a person.
