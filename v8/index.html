A founder walks in · 01

A founder walks in, carrying a knot.

They built fast. AI helped. They have a hundred things and no idea which one to say first.

The noise · 02

AI gave them infinite output.

All of it plausible. None of it pointing anywhere. More is not the problem anymore. Aim is.

One question · 03

I pick up the quill and ask one thing: what does this actually say?

Twenty-five years has taught me the question matters more than the output. The right question does most of the cutting.

The cut · 04

We cut. The knot loosens. One clean thread comes free.

Strike the adjectives. Strike the hedging. Keep the one true thing and the proof behind it.

The line · 05

The thread becomes a line. The line becomes a thing.

One sentence a stranger gets in five seconds. One screen that says what the product does. One deck that earns the next slide.

It ships · 06

It ships. The founder leaves lighter than they came.

That's the whole job: walk in with a knot, walk out with a line.
